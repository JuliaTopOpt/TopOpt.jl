Bibliography

[1]
M. P. Bendsøe and O. Sigmund. Topology Optimization: Theory, Methods, and Applications. 2nd Edition (Springer, 2003). SIMP method (Ch. 1), compliance minimization (§2.1), volume constraint (§2.1), chequerboard filtering (§3.4), thermal/heat conduction topology optimization (§1.3, §4.1).
[2]
A. Iga, S. Nishiwaki, K. Izui and M. Yoshimura. Topology optimization for thermal conductors considering design-dependent effects, including heat conduction and convection. International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer 52, 2721–2732 (2009). Heat conduction topology optimization: SIMP for thermal conductivity, thermal compliance minimization.
[3]
X. Huang and Y.-M. Xie. A further review of ESO type methods for topology optimization. Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization 41, 671–683 (2010).
[4]
M. P. Bendsøe and O. Sigmund. Material interpolation schemes in topology optimization. Archive of Applied Mechanics 69, 635–654 (1999). SIMP power-law interpolation, justification of the penalization exponent.
[5]
K.-J. Bathe. Finite Element Procedures (Prentice-Hall, 1996). Chapter 4.2: Plane Strain and Plane Stress Conditions. Chapter 9.3: Solution of Linearized Buckling Problems.
[6]
P. Duysinx and M. P. Bendsøe. Topology Optimization of Continuum Structures with Local Stress Constraints. International Journal for Numerical Methods in Engineering 43, 1453–1478 (1998). Stress criteria for porous/SIMP materials and epsilon-relaxation of local stress constraints in topology optimization.
[7]
C. Le, J. Norato, T. E. Bruns, C. Ha and D. A. Tortorelli. Stress-based topology optimization for continua. Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization 41, 605–620 (2010). Relaxed stress rho^(1/2) sigma, regional p-norm stress measures with adaptive normalization; L-bracket benchmark.
[8]
M. Bruggi. On an alternative approach to stress constraints relaxation in topology optimization. Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization 36, 125–141 (2008). The qp-approach: stress penalized with exponent q lower than the stiffness exponent p.
[9]
G. D. Cheng and X. Guo, ε-relaxed approach in structural topology optimization. Structural Optimization 13, 258–266 (1997), epsilon-relaxation of stress constraints to make singular optima accessible.
[10]
A. Verbart, M. Langelaar and F. van Keulen. A unified aggregation and relaxation approach for stress-constrained topology optimization. Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization 55, 663–679 (2017). Review of relaxation and aggregation; unified approach in which lower-bound aggregation simultaneously relaxes the feasible domain.
[11]
G. Sved and Z. Ginos. Structural optimization under multiple loading. International Journal of Mechanical Sciences 10, 803–805 (1968). First observation of singular optima in stress-constrained (three-bar truss) optimization.
[12]
M. Stolpe and K. Svanberg. On the trajectories of the epsilon-relaxation approach for stress-constrained truss topology optimization. Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization 21, 140–151 (2001). The global optimum of the epsilon-relaxed problem can be discontinuous in epsilon, so continuation on epsilon does not guarantee convergence to the true optimum.
[13]
H. P. Gavin. Geometric Stiffness Effects in 2D Trusses (2014). CEE 421L lecture notes, Duke University: truss geometric stiffness matrix, eqs. (3)–(4).
[14]
X. Liu, W.-J. Yi, Q. S. Li and P.-S. Shen. Genetic evolutionary structural optimization. Journal of Constructional Steel Research 64, 305–311 (2008).
